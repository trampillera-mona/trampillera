02 / THE IDEA

An independent network of connected perspectives

The project is conceived as a digital ecosystem rather than a single-format publication. It brings together an editorial portal and an immersive 3D environment, creating room for different forms of content, exploration and interaction.

“Different worlds. Connected perspectives.”
03 / ECOSYSTEM

The project’s visible concept references two core names — EZPA and JARVISER — alongside its Classic Portal and 3D experience. Their capabilities and integration continue to be represented by the modules available in the live platform.
