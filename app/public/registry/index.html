02 / Listing types

What belongs here?

Independent deployments

Self-hosted instances and operational deployments maintained by independent teams.

Forks & derivatives

Modified distributions with a distinct identity, documented changes and their own maintainers.

Integrations & extensions

Connectors, plugins, adapters, themes, tools and other interoperable components.

Research & experiments

Prototypes, technical studies, demonstrations and reproducible experiments.

Community services

Documentation, hosting, implementation, training and support offered independently.

Compatible projects

Related projects that interoperate with TRAMPILLERA without claiming to be official components.

03 / Submit a listing

Make your work discoverable.

Submit a project through GitHub. The issue form collects the minimum information needed for a human review. Do not include passwords, API keys, private customer data or confidential infrastructure details.

Describe

Provide a public project URL, maintainer identity, category, license and a concise description.

Review

The registry maintainer checks links, attribution, identity clarity and whether the listing is in scope.

Publish

Accepted entries are added with their independent ownership and verification status clearly stated.
