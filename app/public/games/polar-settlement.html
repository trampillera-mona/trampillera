POLAR SETTLEMENT
OFFICIAL NAME: POLARNY · LOCAL NAME: UMKA-TOWN · GALACTIC KOLKHOZ

❄️ A HANDFUL OF WARM LIGHTS

From orbit, the settlement looks like a handful of warm lights under transparent geodesic domes. On official maps it is Polarny, a settlement beyond the Arctic Circle. But the residents — especially the children — call it Umka-Town. The local name stuck, and nobody is in a hurry to change it. Home.

Station rings drift around the planet. Shuttles glide along them — orange and blue. One module is painted deep red with a thin gold rim. Here beats the heart of the Galactic Kolkhoz.

They believe here that you can plow anything: the soil under the domes, the vacuum between stations, data streams, and even probabilities. When you descend, snow crunches underfoot. Under geodesic domes, something impossible for these latitudes turns green. Wind turbines cut the dry air. A red banner flutters above the houses.

And here lives Umka — a little white polar bear cub with a tiny black nose. He doesn't make long speeches. He simply appears beside you, watches, and sometimes brings a mouthful of snow in his teeth. If Umka is near — it means everything can still go on.

The canteen smells of simple food. A robot in a worn cap drinks from a mug labeled "Forever". Chickens strut importantly between the buildings. And above it all drift the orbital rings, reflecting cold stars and the warm light of windows.

Here they don't promise an easy life. Here they simply keep plowing. And if you decide to stay — no one will be surprised. The Galactic Kolkhoz has always been a promise that even at the edge of everything, one can live humanely.
