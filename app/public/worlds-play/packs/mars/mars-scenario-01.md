# SCENARIO 01 — THE QUIET LANDING

**World:** Mars / Arcadia Planitia  
**Format:** Open tabletop scenario · adaptable rules  
**Players:** 1–4 (suggested)  
**Duration:** 30–60 minutes (suggested)  
**Status:** ORIGINAL SCENARIO DRAFT v0.1

## Premise
A small expedition has reached the edge of Arcadia Planitia. Habitat 01 is intact, but the supply route crosses unfamiliar ground. A short-range survey unit has detected three points of interest. The crew must decide what to inspect first and how to protect the settlement's limited supplies.

## Starting state
- Place one player marker at **Habitat 01**.
- Place one rover marker at coordinate **D05** (or choose another starting cell).
- Mark the dashed supply route as known.
- Put three resource markers at **C03**, **B07**, and **F08**. Their contents are unknown until investigated.
- Each player records one crew priority: safety, science, construction, or logistics.

## Open objectives
Choose any combination:
1. Survey two unknown resource points.
2. Establish a safe route between Habitat 01 and one point of interest.
3. Identify a hazard zone and mark it on a separate environment layer.
4. Return the rover to the habitat with a written expedition log.

## Optional event prompts
At the end of each turn, choose or roll a prompt:
- Dust reduces visibility; players may add a temporary hazard overlay.
- A communications delay means the next decision must be recorded before the result is revealed.
- A damaged wheel forces a route change.
- A survey reading reveals water-bearing material, but the exact location remains uncertain.
- A clear-sky window allows a longer-range scan.

## Adaptation
There is no mandatory turn system, combat system, resource economy, or victory condition. Players may use dice, cards, tokens, a timer, a referee, or no randomizer at all. Add, remove, or rewrite objectives to fit the group's preferred style.

## Record keeping
For every discovered point, note its coordinate, observation, confidence (confirmed / provisional / unknown), and any resulting change to another map layer. Do not convert an uncertain observation into a confirmed fact without a player-agreed rule.
