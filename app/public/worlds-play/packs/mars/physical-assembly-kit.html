02 / CREW ROLE CARDS

Optional roles. One player may hold more than one role; groups may rename or replace them.

ROLE-01

SAFETY LEAD

Tracks crew exposure, route risk, shelter decisions and unresolved hazards.

ROLE-02

SURVEY LEAD

Records observations and distinguishes confirmed findings from hypotheses.

ROLE-03

CONSTRUCTION LEAD

Plans habitat extensions, landing-area improvements and repair priorities.

ROLE-04

LOGISTICS LEAD

Tracks supplies, travel priorities, caches and return plans.

ROLE-05

EXPEDITION RECORDER

Maintains the log, preserves earlier entries and updates the appropriate map layer.

ROLE-06

OPEN ROLE

Invent a specialist role that fits the group's chosen story and play style.

Evidence discipline: use KNOWN, PROVISIONAL, UNKNOWN, CHANGED or DISPUTED. A marker is not proof of a resource, safe route or discovered object. UNVERIFIED ≠ FALSE.
