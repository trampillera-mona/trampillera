# ORIGAMI FIELD MARKER — BEGINNER FOLDING GUIDE

**Model:** Standing triangular field marker  
**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Paper:** One square sheet, ideally 120–160 mm per side  
**Tools:** None; no cutting or glue  
**Use:** A reusable tabletop marker for a rover, waypoint, hazard, resource, or objective  
**Status:** Original folding sequence draft v0.1

## Prepare
Choose a colour or draw a symbol on one side of the square. Start with the coloured or marked side facing down.

## Fold
1. Fold the square in half diagonally to make a triangle. Crease firmly, then unfold.
2. Fold along the other diagonal and unfold. The two creases should cross at the centre.
3. Turn the paper over. Fold the square in half horizontally, crease, and unfold.
4. Bring the left and right edges toward the centre line. Let the existing creases guide the paper inward.
5. Collapse the sheet into a compact triangular base along the creases. Press the layers flat.
6. Stand the model on its lower folded edge and gently open the two front layers to form a stable tent-like marker. Adjust the base so it stands upright.

## Customize
- Draw a rover, habitat, resource, hazard, or waypoint symbol on the visible face.
- Use different colours for different player roles or map layers.
- Write the map coordinate on the reverse.
- If the paper does not stand, use a slightly heavier square or widen the base folds.

## Table use
Place the marker on the matching map coordinate. A marker can be moved, rotated, or re-labelled as the scenario evolves. It is a physical aid only; it does not impose any rules or numerical values.

## Note
This is an original beginner paper-fold marker concept. Folding results vary with paper weight and crease accuracy. Test with scrap paper before making a full set.
