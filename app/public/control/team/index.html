Mission design principles

Good missions have a clear purpose, a visible result and a cooperative path. Examples: map an undocumented workflow, test a prototype, improve a shared knowledge page, prepare a verified research brief, or complete a learning module together.
Do not assign XP to attendance, obedience, hours online or subjective loyalty. Use it for completed learning and documented collaboration.
