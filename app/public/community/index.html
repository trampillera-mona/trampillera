01 / Distribution model

Build with it. Make it your own.

The project welcomes independent deployments, forks, experiments, integrations and commercial derivatives under the terms of the MIT License. You do not need to ask permission to do what the license already allows.

01 — ACCESS

Inspect and learn

Read the source, understand the architecture, test the application and adapt it to your own environment.

02 — CREATE

Fork and extend

Change the code, add capabilities, connect services or create a distinct product for a different audience.

03 — DISTRIBUTE

Share and commercialize

Redistribute original or modified code, including commercially, while meeting the MIT notice requirements and other applicable terms.

02 / Shared expectations

Freedom works best with clarity.

An open license gives broad permissions. Clear attribution and honest identity help users understand what they are installing and who is responsible for it.

Keep the noticesRetain applicable copyright and MIT License notices with redistributed copies.
Identify your changesExplain when a deployment is modified, independently maintained or materially different.
Choose your own identityUse a distinct name, domain, logo and product identity for your independent project.
Do not imply endorsementDo not suggest that TRAMPILLERA operates, certifies, endorses or supports an independent fork.
Respect third-party termsDependencies, fonts, media, libraries and other assets may carry separate license obligations.
Be precise about capabilityDescribe what you tested and verified. Do not present a prototype or untested integration as certified.
